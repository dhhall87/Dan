# Passing privacy policy

*Draft, November 2026. Before publishing: add your name or company and a contact email where marked, and host this page at a public address.*

Passing saves where you are when you tap, so you can look up what you passed later. This page says what it keeps and what leaves your iPhone.

## What Passing collects

Nothing. Passing has no account, no ads, no analytics and no server. The developer never receives your pins or any other data from the app.

## What stays on your iPhone

- **Pins:** where you were, when, your direction of travel, the side and distance you tapped, names, notes, photos, groups and the town.
- **Settings**, including the Work and Home places you may set for review reminders.
- **Apple Watch pins** stay on the watch until Passing on your iPhone has saved them, and go directly between the two.

Like any app's data, this is included in your own iPhone backups.

## What leaves your iPhone, and when

- **Apple Maps:** a pin's location is sent to Apple to find its town name (you can turn this off in Settings → Privacy), and when you open a map, Look Around, "What's here?" or a road route. Apple's privacy policy covers those requests.
- **Siri:** what you say to Siri is handled by Siri under your iPhone's settings. Passing's own "Listen after tap" recognizes speech on your iPhone only.
- **Things you share:** an export, a backup or a map link goes only where you send it. A Google Maps link gives Google that location.

## Permissions

- **Location (While Using):** to stamp a pin. Passing never tracks you in the background.
- **Microphone and Speech Recognition:** only if you turn on Listen after tap.
- **Camera:** only when you take a photo for a pin.
- **Notifications:** only if you turn on review reminders or the number on the icon.

## Keeping and deleting

Pins move to Recently Deleted after 7 days unless you keep them, and are erased 30 days later. Settings → Privacy → Delete All Pins erases everything at once. Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

## Children

Passing is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
