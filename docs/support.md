## Common questions

**A pin says "No location."** Passing saves the pin even when GPS has no fresh reading (in a garage or tunnel, or with Location turned off). Check Settings → Advanced → Location access, and that Precise Location is on for Passing in iOS Settings.

**The widget doesn't show in CarPlay.** CarPlay shows small widgets on iOS 26 or later, in cars with a touchscreen. Add the Pin or Pin by Side widget in CarPlay's widget settings on the iPhone.

**Siri doesn't understand.** Say "Pin that in Passing" or "Mark this in Passing". Open Passing once after installing so iOS learns the phrases.

**My pins disappeared.** Pins move to Recently Deleted after 7 days unless you star them. Open Pins → Recently Deleted to recover them within 30 days, or turn on Keep records indefinitely in Settings.

**Moving to a new iPhone.** An iPhone backup carries your pins. You can also use Settings → Back Up and Restore to save one file and restore it on the new phone.

**Passing spoke, or stayed quiet, when it shouldn't.** Passing follows the Silent switch. If that goes wrong, please write with what happened.

**Please use Passing safely.** Mount your phone, use Siri or the CarPlay widget where you can, and review pins only when parked.
